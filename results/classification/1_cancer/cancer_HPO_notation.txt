================================================================================
                         OBJECTIVE FORMULA
================================================================================

  Objective = 0.4 × F + 0.4 × U − 0.2 × P

  Where:
    F (Fidelity)     = avg(KS_Similarity, Correlation_Similarity)
                       How statistically similar is the synthetic data to real?
                       Range: [0, 1], higher = better

    U (Utility)      = clip(TSTR_F1 / TRTR_F1, 0, 1)
                       Can a model trained on synthetic data perform as well as
                       one trained on real data?
                       Range: [0, 1], higher = better

    P (Privacy Risk) = composite of MIA_AUC, NNDR
                       How much does the synthetic data leak about the training
                       set? Membership inference attack success rate.
                       Range: [0, 1], higher = MORE risky (penalized)

  Sub-metrics:
    KS Similarity      1 − Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance, averaged across columns
    Corr Similarity    Correlation matrix similarity (real vs synthetic)
    MIA AUC            Membership Inference Attack AUC (higher = riskier)
    NNDR               Nearest Neighbor Distance Ratio (closer to 1 = more unique)
    DCR                Distance to Closest Record (higher = more private)
    TSTR F1            Train-on-Synthetic, Test-on-Real F1 score
    TRTR F1            Train-on-Real, Test-on-Real F1 score (ceiling)
    F1 Gap             TRTR_F1 − TSTR_F1 (smaller = better)

================================================================================
                        KEY OBSERVATIONS
================================================================================

  1. TVAE and CTGAN dominate — both achieve high fidelity AND utility.
     TVAE edges out CTGAN due to lower privacy risk.

  2. ForestDiffusion and WGAN-GP produce the most realistic-looking data
     (highest fidelity, lowest privacy risk) but generate data with ZERO
     downstream ML utility. The synthetic data doesn't preserve decision
     boundaries.

  3. CopulaGAN and CTAB-GAN+ have high privacy risk (MIA AUC > 0.97),
     meaning a membership inference attack can almost perfectly detect
     training records — a concern for sensitive data.

  4. GaussianCopula is the fastest generator (30s) and makes a good
     baseline, though utility lags behind GAN-based methods.

  5. Longer training (more epochs) consistently helped across generators
     that support it. Smaller batch sizes also tended to improve results.
